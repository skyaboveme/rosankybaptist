Rosanky Baptist Church helps [Disciple Learner Resources](https://www.disciplelearner.org) by volunteering at a local Back to School event, giving away hundreds of Bibles, Bible Comic Books from [Gospel Tract Society](https://www.gospeltractsociety.org), Bible Planners, praying for people, and more. Rosanky Baptist Church also invited their contact from the [Gideons](https://gideons.org/), who brought more volunteers along with additional boxes of Bibles.

We would love for YOU to join us next time in sharing God’s Word to this next generation!
